The Making

From a line to a legacy

  1. One temple necklace, from the first pencil line to the final polish. Scroll to watch it made.

  2. 01 / 04 · Sketch

    It begins on paper

    Peacocks, a seated goddess and a temple tower are drawn and balanced by hand, line by line, before any gold is touched.

  3. 02 / 04 · Cast

    The drawing becomes gold

    Each feather and temple step is raised in relief. Fresh from the mould, the gold is still dull and unfinished.

  4. 03 / 04 · Set

    One drop at a time

    Green drops and seed pearls are hung along the fringe by hand, so the collar moves as she does.

  5. 04 / 04 · Polish

    Brought to a glow

    The raised gold is polished bright while the carving keeps its antique shadow, so every detail reads from across a room.
